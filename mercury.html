What value means here

A player is worth the points he scores above the last man you could start at his position. The lineup sets that baseline, and this lineup is unusual.

Points above replacement by rank within each position. Three receiver slots keep wideouts valuable deep into the pool. One quarterback slot flattens the position almost immediately — past the elite few, a passer is worth nothing you could not stream.

Two sources, one ranking

Expert consensus decides who is better. Blended projections from ESPN and Sleeper decide by how much, measured in points above replacement so the two can be averaged despite different season lengths.
