Column key ▾ what each number means
  • Astro #
    This model's overall board — every player re-ranked by Value for this league. Rank 1 is the best pick available.
  • Value
    What he is worth as a draft pick: projected points above the last startable player at his position. Compare across positions — a WR at 80 and a QB at 80 are equally good picks. This is what the board sorts, drafts and simulates on. By default it is half projection, half expert consensus; the Value weighting buttons above the table change that mix, and Astro # and Edge move with it.
  • Pts
    Best estimate of the fantasy points he actually scores this season in Astroworld scoring — full PPR, 6-point passing TDs, −2 per INT. ESPN and Sleeper projections blended, 17-game basis. No expert opinion in it. Only comparable within a position — every starting QB outscores every RB, so sorting on it stacks quarterbacks at the top.
  • Pts+
    The same projection measured from the last startable player at his position instead of from zero — which makes it comparable across positions. Sorting on it gives you a real draft board with no expert opinion in it. Use the Pts: season total / over replacement button above the table to switch. Green means the projections rate him above the consensus, red below; hover a number for the other view.
  • ADP
    Average draft position in real PPR redraft leagues, from Sleeper — roughly where the room will take him.
  • FP rank
    FantasyPros overall consensus rank. A market ordering, not a value scale — and it is built for generic scoring, so it under-rates what this league pays for.
  • Edge
    Value minus the best player still on the board after his ADP. Positive means he is a bargain at his market price; negative means waiting gets you more. The single most actionable column on draft day.
  • Bye
    Bye week. Watch for three or more starters sharing one.
