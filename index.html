How the score works

A pick's worth falls off a cliff after the first couple of rounds, so surplus has to be measured on that curve — not in raw picks.

The value curve

Both the player and the pick he costs are converted to points on the same decay curve, then subtracted. Elite players sit on the steep part, which is why a top-12 name at pick 50 dwarfs a top-50 name at pick 110.

V(n) = 1000 × (2.5 / (n + 1.5))^0.80 Keeper Score = V(FantasyPros rank) − V(keeper pick)

Why not just subtract picks?

Raw "picks gained" treats pick 1 and pick 60 as 59 units apart, the same as pick 100 and pick 159. They are not remotely the same. The exponent (0.80) is what makes the top of the board scarce.
