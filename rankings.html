What "value" means here

The same engine that drives the keeper ratings, exposed on its own so you can draft off it.

Points above replacement

Astroworld starts QB / WR / WR / WR / RB / RB / TE / flex across 12 teams. That lineup decides how deep each position runs before you are into waiver-wire bodies — and that depth is what a player's value is measured against.

Why it disagrees with the consensus

FantasyPros ranks for a generic PPR league. Two things about this one push players around:

  • 1
    Three WR slots. Receivers stay startable down to about WR43, so a mid-tier WR is easy to replace and worth less than his overall rank suggests.
  • 2
    One TE slot with a cliff. After the top handful, tight end falls off a ledge — which makes the good ones quietly scarce.
  • 3
    One QB slot. Even with 6-point passing TDs, QB13 scores nearly what QB6 does. Past the elite few a quarterback is streamable.

Column key

  • Astro #
    This model's overall board — every player re-ranked by Value for this league. Rank 1 is the best pick available.
  • Off
    Team offense, from the market. Kalshi's implied win total for his team, tilted by the real-money books on which team scores the most and the fewest points, scaled 0-100 across the league. Bright green is an elite environment; dark red is a wasteland. A tiebreaker between two similar players, not a projection of his own production — a great back on a bad team still gets the carries. Hover the chip for the numbers behind it.
  • Value
    What he is worth as a draft pick: projected points above the last startable player at his position. Compare across positions — a WR at 80 and a QB at 80 are equally good picks. This is what the board sorts, drafts and simulates on. By default it is half projection, half expert consensus; the Value weighting buttons above the table change that mix, and Astro # and Edge move with it.
  • Pts
    Best estimate of the fantasy points he actually scores this season in Astroworld scoring — full PPR, 6-point passing TDs, −2 per INT. ESPN and Sleeper projections blended, 17-game basis. No expert opinion in it. Only comparable within a position — every starting QB outscores every RB, so sorting on it stacks quarterbacks at the top.
  • Pts+
    The same projection measured from the last startable player at his position instead of from zero — which makes it comparable across positions. Sorting on it gives you a real draft board with no expert opinion in it. Use the Pts: season total / over replacement button above the table to switch. Green means the projections rate him above the consensus, red below; hover a number for the other view.
  • ADP
    Average draft position in real PPR redraft leagues, from Sleeper — roughly where the room will take him.
  • FP rank
    FantasyPros overall consensus rank. A market ordering, not a value scale — and it is built for generic scoring, so it under-rates what this league pays for.
  • Edge
    Value minus the best player still on the board after his ADP. Positive means he is a bargain at his market price; negative means waiting gets you more. The single most actionable column on draft day.
  • Bye
    Bye week. Watch for three or more starters sharing one.
  • $ tag
    A Vegas adjustment. Kalshi runs real-money markets on season stat thresholds ("4,000+ passing yards?"). Where a player's market is liquid and brackets the 50% line, that money-backed median is compared with the projections and the projection is moved halfway toward it, capped at ±20 points. Only ~25 players have markets liquid enough to count. The money runs a few percent below the projections almost everywhere — it prices missed games, projections don't. Hover the tag for the exact stat.
